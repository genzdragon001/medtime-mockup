💊 What this simulates

Full clickable flow of the MedTime Android app from your proposal — onboarding, medication setup, the loud reminder alert, camera-based dose verification, and offline SMS caregiver alerts. Everything runs locally in this page; no install needed.

🎯 Core features from the manuscript

100% offline & no account · loud audio + vibration alerts · automatic pill inventory · mandatory camera photo proof (no faked taps) · caregiver monitoring via offline SMS.
